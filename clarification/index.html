Author’s Preface on Contextual Tone: 
"This document includes a structural tension — between permissive authorship and intellectual protection.
I have released RCR and Lucidism openly, not to gatekeep but to document. 
Yet in this piece, you may hear a defensive tone — born not of ego, but of necessity. If you sense contradiction, you are correct.  But it is recursive, not rhetorical.
You may see this as vanity.
But it was written when silence became complicity.

If you understand recursion, then you already know:
This text corrects itself. Not with force. But with light."
— M. X. Theodore
